## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

Our idea is to create an online platform where farmers can sell their products directly to customers and shop owners without depending on middlemen. Farmers can upload details about their products, quantity and price. Customers can easily view the products and buy what they need. This will make buying and selling easier for both farmers and customers.

2. How It Addresses the Problem

Nowadays, farmers sometimes do not get a fair price for their hard work, while customers have to pay higher prices. Our solution helps to reduce this problem by connecting farmers and customers directly. It helps farmers earn better money and allows customers to buy fresh products at reasonable prices. It can also help reduce food waste by making sure products reach the right people at the right time.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

The special thing about our idea is that it brings farmers and customers closer through a single platform. Instead of depending on many people to sell their products, farmers can directly reach their buyers. It also helps farmers understand how much people need their products and plan deliveries easily. Our main aim is to support farmers, save customers' money and make the selling and buying process simple and helpful for everyone.

## Technical Approach

1. Technologies to Be Used

We plan to create a simple website or mobile app where farmers and customers can connect with each other. Farmers can add their products, prices and available quantities. Customers can view the products and order them easily. We will use a simple and user-friendly design so that everyone can understand and use it without difficulty.

2. Method and Process

First, farmers will register and add the details of the products they want to sell. Then, customers can visit the platform, check the available products and select what they need. After receiving an order, farmers can prepare the products for delivery. Finally, the products will reach the customers directly. This process will make buying and selling easier, faster and more convenient for everyone.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

Our idea is practical and can be developed with the help of a simple website or mobile app. Nowadays, many people use mobile phones and the internet, so farmers and customers can easily use this platform. It does not require a big setup to start. We can begin with a small number of farmers and customers and slowly expand it.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

Some farmers may not know how to use online platforms. Internet connection may also be a problem in some villages. Sometimes, delivery delays, product quality and payment issues may happen. These are some of the challenges we may face while developing this idea.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

To overcome these problems, we can make the platform very simple and easy to understand. We can also provide basic guidance to farmers on how to use it. We will try to make the delivery process smooth and ensure that customers receive good-quality products. Proper communication between farmers and customers can also help avoid misunderstandings.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential Impact on the Target Audience

Our idea mainly helps farmers, customers and small shop owners. Farmers can sell their products directly and get a better price for their hard work. Customers can buy fresh products at reasonable prices. It also gives small shop owners an easier way to find and purchase products directly from farmers.

2. Benefits of the Solution

- Social Benefits: It creates a better connection between farmers and customers.
- Economic Benefits: Farmers can earn more money, and customers can save money.
- Environmental Benefits: It can help reduce food waste by making products reach customers on time.
- Overall Benefits: It makes buying and selling easier and supports farmers and local businesses.
